## Key Photography Rules for Your Bottles

1. **Light backgrounds make juice colours POP** — white marble, light wood especially for product shots
2. **Natural light preferred** — avoid harsh shadows on plastic
3. **Slight backlight** — shows the juice colour glowing through the plastic
4. **The black lid is a brand asset** — photograph it close-up to signal sealed freshness
5. **Never use glass or metal cap references in AI prompts** — always specify "clear plastic bottle with black screw-top lid"
