- **BLUE (Startup Ideas)**: #3B82F6 → #2563EB
- **RED (Pain Points)**: #EF4444 → #DC2626
- **GOLD (Aha Moments)**: #F59E0B → #D97706

**Posting Strategy**:
- Frequency: 1 post every 12-18 hours
- Best times: 8 AM EST, 2 PM EST, 8 PM EST (rotate)
- Total rollout: 8-10 weeks
