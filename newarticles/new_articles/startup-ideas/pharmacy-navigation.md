Pharmacy navigation has become a consumer problem masquerading as a healthcare issue. People spend hours on hold, drive to multiple locations, fight insurance battles, and delay care because they can't navigate a system that was designed for efficiency, not patients. A dedicated navigation platform could solve this.

The opportunity is a service that handles pharmacy logistics end-to-end. Users upload their prescriptions and insurance information, and the platform finds pharmacies with stock, compares prices, handles insurance authorization, manages transfers between locations, and provides real-time updates on status. For recurring medications, automate refills and delivery coordination.

Key differentiators: transparent pricing before you commit, real-time inventory across multiple chains, insurance coverage verification upfront, and human support for complex cases. The platform should become the single point of contact for all pharmacy needs, shielding users from the complexity of interacting with multiple systems.

The business model: freemium for individuals (pay per complex case), subscription for chronic medication management, and partnerships with healthcare providers who want to reduce patient friction. The real value is in the data—understanding where the pharmacy system breaks down for different patient populations.

Market validation is everywhere: people describe pharmacy interactions as a second job, chronic patients develop elaborate systems to manage medications, and healthcare providers spend time they don't have helping patients navigate pharmacy logistics.

**Why This Works Now:** Pharmacy staffing crises, insurance complexity, and the rise of chronic conditions have created a perfect storm of demand for better navigation.
