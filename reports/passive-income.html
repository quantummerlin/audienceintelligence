🔥 Top Pain Points

#1 FINANCIAL STRESS

Financial Stress Is the Root Cause

Money problems are fueling 60%+ of toxic relationship dynamics. People describe being trapped in debt spirals, unable to save, and feeling like the entire system is broken for parents, especially when kids are sick.

"People are bleeding financially in ways they don't notice — lifestyle creep, subscription creep, social comparison spending"
"Using debt like it's fake money — people spend up to 83% more when using credit vs cash"
#2 FAMILY DYNAMICS

Family Financial Abuse Is Widespread

Partners controlling finances, parents exploiting children's credit, and family members guilt-tripping for refusing support. These dynamics create long-term financial and emotional damage that prevents people from building independent lives.

"Family members exploiting elderly parents financially — siblings living rent-free while parents pay for everything"
"People feel guilty about not sacrificing for family due to conditioning"
#3 PASSIVE INCOME

"Passive" Income Isn't Actually Passive

People frustrated by schemes that pay pennies and require active work. SaaS "passive income" requires 20+ hours/week — support tickets, maintenance, marketing never stop.

"SaaS 'passive income' requires 20+ hours/week of active work"
"People frustrated by lack of truly passive income opportunities"
#4 HEALTHCARE

Healthcare Costs Are Crippling

People struggle with high costs, lack of access, and feeling the system prioritizes profits over care. Healthcare costs are a leading cause of bankruptcy and prevent people from seeking necessary care.

"High healthcare costs and physician salaries being too high"
"Medical debt and inability to afford healthcare"
#5 JOB MARKET

Job Market Instability & Wage Stagnation

People describe being overworked, underpaid, and facing unrealistic experience requirements. Big 4 audit work is unsustainable with 60+ hour weeks, 12-hour shifts, and zero training. Wage theft and productivity-wage gap costing workers $50 trillion since 1975.

"Big 4 audit work is unsustainable with 60+ hour weeks, 12-hour shifts, and zero training"
"CS majors struggling to find jobs despite degree"
#6 HOUSING

Housing Costs Consume Everything

People face constant rent increases, poor maintenance, and difficulty qualifying despite higher income. Housing costs consume 40-50% of income for many, leaving no room for savings or investment.

"Landlords raising rents while properties remain in poor condition"
"Financial struggles preventing people from qualifying for housing despite higher income"
#7 AI ANXIETY

AI Anxiety & Job Displacement Fears

Widespread worry about AI replacing jobs and creating economic insecurity. This anxiety drives demand for upskilling tools and career transition services.

"AI agents can build complete personal profiles from public data in 15 minutes, creating massive privacy risks"
"AI replacing human labor causing skill atrophy"
#8 TOXIC WORK

Toxic Workplace Cultures

People describe bullying bosses, lack of HR recourse, and environments where employees are treated like children. This drives turnover and mental health issues, creating demand for better management tools.

"Boss yelling and threatening to fire employees for eating lunch"
"Boss making fun of employees for not affording things"
#9 CHILDCARE

Childcare Costs Are a Poverty Trap

Single parents can't work due to childcare costs, and parents feel overwhelmed by expectations. Childcare costs are a major barrier to workforce participation and financial independence.

"Childcare costs making it impossible for single parents to work"
"Parents feel overwhelmed by the expectation to create perfect childhood experiences"
#10 SOCIAL ISOLATION

Social Isolation & Dating Difficulties

Difficulty meeting people, online dating being "enshittified," and feeling abandoned after divorce. Social isolation contributes to mental health issues and prevents people from building support networks.

"Dating as a heterosexual man is extremely difficult due to structural gender imbalances"
"Online dating platforms are 'enshittified' with women receiving vastly more attention than men"
