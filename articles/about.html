Why this exists

Most market research is broken. Surveys are answered by people trying to give "good" answers. Focus groups reward people who talk loudest. Trend reports recycle each other. By the time research reaches you, it's already stale and sanitised.

Online communities are different. When someone posts at 2am about the thing that's genuinely frustrating them, they aren't performing for a researcher. They're just trying to make sense of something real. That's the raw material Aether Intelligence works with.

The goal is simple: take tens of thousands of those unfiltered posts, find the repeating patterns, quantify the intensity, and turn it into something actionable — for product builders, content creators, marketers, and researchers who need to understand what people actually want.

The approach

Every report starts with collection: pulling high-engagement posts from communities where people self-select to discuss a topic in depth. No bots, no incentivised responses, no sampling bias from who answered an email survey. Just real people, talking to other real people, about things that actually matter to them.

From there: pattern extraction. What pain points appear most frequently? What language do people use repeatedly? Where is genuine emotional investment showing up? What questions go unanswered by existing products? What do people keep asking for that doesn't exist yet?

The output is a structured analysis — not a raw dump, not a summary, but a signal map: ranked, quoted, evidenced, and ready to be acted on.

What the reports are used for

Aether Intelligence reports are used by founders validating product ideas, content creators building audience-first strategies, marketers writing copy rooted in real customer language, and researchers who need a fast, reliable pulse on a community they're entering.

Unlike generic trend reports, every analysis here is niche-specific, community-sourced, and updated as new data becomes available. The depth comes from volume — and from asking the right questions of the data.

Custom research

Public reports cover topics chosen by the research agenda. But if your specific niche, product category, or target community isn't covered yet, custom reports are available. You define the topic and the questions you need answered — and we do the collection, extraction, and analysis for you.

What we stand for

Signal over noise

Every output is filtered for what matters. No filler, no padding, no manufactured insights. Only patterns that appear with enough frequency and emotional weight to be actionable.

Source transparency

Reports include methodology notes, source community context, and honest caveats about sample size and limitations. You know exactly where the data came from.

Privacy by design

No personal identifying information is retained, published, or shared. Analysis is conducted at the pattern level, not the individual level.

Honest about what we don't know

Correlation is noted as correlation. Anecdote is noted as anecdote. When sample sizes are small, that's stated. This isn't journalism — it's pattern research.
