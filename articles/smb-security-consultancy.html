The Opportunity

Small-to-medium businesses suffer catastrophic losses because basic security practices are ignored. A county sheriff's office getting crypto-locked because end users had admin rights is not an edge case-it's representative of how widespread security negligence is among SMBs.

These organizations lack dedicated IT staff and don't understand the risks until it's too late. A consultancy providing proactive protection against ransomware and data loss, specifically designed for SMB constraints, addresses a critical gap.

Validation Evidence

The data shows the scale of the problem:

"I got a service call that our app couldn't connect to the DB. I was the one who got to inform the customer that their entire DB server (for a county sheriff's office) had been crypto-lockered because their old IT guy never took admin rights away from end users (Patty in HR just HAD to have those pretty mouse pointers or screensavers)."

Key validation signals:

  • A government entity (county sheriff's office) vulnerable due to basic misconfiguration
  • The breach was preventable with basic security hygiene
  • Even critical infrastructure operates with security practices that should have been eliminated decades ago

Business Model

Monthly retainer for continuous protection

Core services:

  • Continuous monitoring and alerting
  • Patch management and vulnerability scanning
  • Employee security training and phishing simulations
  • Incident response planning and testing
  • Backup verification and disaster recovery

Pricing tiers:

  • Starter: $299/month (basic monitoring + critical patches)
  • Professional: $699/month (full monitoring + training + incident response)
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing (dedicated security analyst + on-site support)

Defensibility

Customized security stacks create switching costs

  • Built around each client's specific software and workflows
  • Rapid incident response reputation builds trust
  • Industry-specific compliance templates (healthcare, government, retail)
  • Employee training tailored to organization culture

Hard to replicate because deep understanding of SMB workflows and constraints is required. Generic enterprise security solutions don't work for organizations without dedicated IT teams.

Risk / Challenge

Sales resistance: SMBs often undervalue security until after a breach. They view it as a cost center, not insurance.

Solution: Lead with case studies and ROI calculations. Show the cost of breach vs. cost of prevention. Use fear responsibly-show that it's not if, but when.

Resource constraints: Small clients can't afford dedicated security personnel. The service must be highly automated to be profitable at SMB price points.

Liability concerns: If a breach happens despite your services, legal exposure is real. Clear contracts, realistic expectations, and cyber insurance partnerships are essential.

What This Means For You

If you're a founder: This is a massive market with minimal competition. Most security firms target enterprises. SMBs are underserved and vulnerable. The barrier to entry is low but the execution required is high-you need to balance automation with human expertise.

If you're an SMB owner: You're operating without adequate security. The risk isn't theoretical-it's inevitable. Basic protections like admin rights management, patching, and backups are not optional anymore.

If you're a security professional: There's enormous opportunity here. Enterprise security is saturated. SMB security is a blue ocean with growing demand and minimal supply.
