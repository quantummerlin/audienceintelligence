The Lab

Founded as a non-profit research lab in 2015 with the explicit mission of building safe artificial general intelligence, OpenAI now operates as a capped-profit subsidiary valued north of $500B. The structural pivot was inevitable: aligning research progress with revenue means shipping product. ChatGPT, released in late 2022, became the consumer interface that defined the modern AI era.

The 2026 strategy is breadth. GPT-5.x covers general use, the o-series (folded into GPT-5.4 Think) handles slow reasoning, GPT-Image-2 owns the text-in-image category, Sora 2 owns video. Distribution comes from the Microsoft alliance plus a still-growing consumer subscription base. No rival ships a model line this wide.

The catch is the question still hanging over the company. Sam Altman's leadership through the November 2023 board crisis, the parade of senior researchers leaving for Anthropic and elsewhere, the public scrutiny on training data and labour practices — OpenAI's product distribution is undisputed, but its long-term moat against Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and the open-source frontier is not.

GPT-5.4 Think

Reasoning

The slow-thinking option, descended from the original o-series. Outpaces every other reasoning model on AIME 2025 and SWE-bench Verified. Expensive per token. Reserve it for problems where wrong answers cost real money — not for daily chat.

GPT-Image-2

Image

Best in class for images that need legible text baked into the composition. The Nano Banana family from Google is faster and cheaper for generic image generation, but for posters, infographics, marketing collateral, or any composition where letters must render correctly, GPT-Image-2 is the sticky pick.

When to Pick OpenAI · When to Pick Someone Else

✓ Pick OpenAI when

  • You need the broadest plugin and feature ecosystem available
  • Image generation requires legible baked-in text (GPT-Image-2)
  • Voice mode for real-time conversational AI is in scope
  • Microsoft alliance shortens the enterprise procurement path
  • You're shipping to consumers who already know ChatGPT

✕ Look elsewhere for

  • Long-context document analysis — Anthropic Claude wins on 500K+ tokens
  • Self-hosted or air-gapped workloads — open weights from Meta or DeepSeek
  • Cost-sensitive high-volume — DeepSeek and Qwen are dramatically cheaper
  • European data residency — Mistral
  • Real-time web search with citations — Perplexity
