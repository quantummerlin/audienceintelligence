### 1. Financial Stress and Money Problems
**Frequency & Intensity:** ~380 upvotes on wage theft and cost of living complaints / ~60% of middle-income earners increase discretionary spending as soon as income rises
**What it is:** Users describe being trapped in debt spirals, unable to save, and feeling like the entire system is broken for parents, especially when kids are sick.
**Evidence:**
> "People are bleeding financially in ways they don't notice — lifestyle creep, subscription creep, social comparison spending"
> "Using debt like it's fake money — people spend up to 83% more when using credit vs cash"
**Why it matters:** Financial stress creates cascading problems from mental health issues to relationship breakdowns and inability to invest in future.

### 2. Toxic Relationships and Family Dysfunction
**Frequency & Intensity:** ~380 upvotes on relationship pain / ~30+ comments on family financial abuse
**What it is:** Partners controlling finances, parents exploiting children's credit, and family members guilt-tripping for refusing support.
