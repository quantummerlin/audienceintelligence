Gap #01
Bank 2FA — Locking Out Older Devices
Fintech 610 signal High Opportunity
"A few weeks back they decided to lock anyone who didn't have the newest iOS version installed on their phone out 'until they update'."
What's Missing
Accessible authentication that works on older devices or offers fallback methods (SMS, hardware tokens, web-based verification) without forcing hardware upgrades. Disproportionately affects low-income, elderly, and rural users.
The Opportunity
A 2FA platform that supports multiple fallback methods alongside app-based auth, with automatic degradation to accessible alternatives for devices that can't run the latest OS — no user left behind by a software update cycle they can't control.
Gap #02
Wedding Tech — Subscription Model Mismatch
Events 500 signal Validated Gap
"Nobody wants to pay monthly for something they need for 6 months. We almost launched with a SaaS model. Switched to one-time event passes ($69–$149) and conversions jumped immediately."
What's Missing
Pricing models aligned with short-term, event-based needs. Existing platforms treat weddings like ongoing subscriptions when they're single-event purchases. The market has validated the fix — founders just need to adopt it.
The Opportunity
Wedding-focused tools offered as one-time event passes or tiered packages tied to the event date. The conversion improvement is already documented — this is an execution gap, not a discovery gap.
Gap #03
Subscription Creep — No Perpetual License Option
Consumer 110 signal Emerging Gap
"Entire things you used to own are now leased to you for more money and your private data — software, music, video games, features in your new car, etc. It's actually shameful, but difficult to fight."
What's Missing
Transparent ownership options or fair-value lease-to-own alternatives that respect user control and data privacy. No mainstream marketplace exists for perpetual licenses or opt-out data harvesting.
The Opportunity
A marketplace or licensing layer that lets users purchase perpetual licenses for software/media or buy out car feature leases after a set period, with explicit data-use guarantees. An "anti-subscription" brand positioning could command significant premium.
Gap #04
Customer Service Training — Panic-Prone Instructors
B2B Tools 20 signal Niche Gap
"From day one of training, there were constant technical issues. Every single day someone had system problems. Instead of handling the situation calmly and professionally, the trainer would panic and get frustrated."
What's Missing
Reliable, low-tech training environments and instructors trained in de-escalation. Current platforms simulate perfect conditions but not real-world failure scenarios. Instructors aren't coached on composure under technical failure.
The Opportunity
A customer service training platform that deliberately simulates technical failures, coaches instructors on de-escalation protocols, and scores trainers on composure — not just content delivery. The training for the trainers is the gap.
Gap #05
Manufacturer Communication — Unilateral Design Changes
B2B Tools 9 signal Niche Gap
"When I asked my supplier, they said it's because these details are difficult to produce. Well I understand there's a gap between design and production, while the point is, they didn't ask me anything before they made this decision!"
What's Missing
Proactive collaboration loops where manufacturers seek client approval before altering prototypes. No standard change-request workflow exists between small clients and overseas manufacturers — the power imbalance is built into the process.
The Opportunity
A prototype-management tool that enforces change-request workflows, version control, and automated client notifications for any deviation from spec — essentially GitHub for physical product development between founders and manufacturers.
Gap #06
Smart Locks — No Tenant-Only Privacy Mode
PropTech 900 signal High Opportunity
"If they have access to the app, they can remotely lock/unlock the door. As well as see when the door is locked/unlocked. Essentially trolling to see when you come and go. I'd be pissed."
What's Missing
Tenant-controlled access logs and the ability to opt out of landlord remote management while maintaining security. Current smart lock systems are designed for property owner control, not tenant privacy rights.
The Opportunity
Smart locks with a verified tenant-only mode that disables remote landlord access and provides transparent, real-time access logs visible only to the tenant. A tenant-rights positioning in the smart home market that no current player occupies.
Gap #07
Remote Job Boards — Scam Signal vs. Legitimate Work
HR & Hiring 250 signal High Opportunity
"I see a lot of advice about remote work and online skills, but it's hard to know what actually helps you get real work."
What's Missing
Curated, vetted job listings that signal legitimacy, with clear skill-to-job matching for entry-level candidates. Current boards are flooded with scams, and legitimate listings are indistinguishable from fraudulent ones to new market entrants.
The Opportunity
A job board that verifies employers, highlights entry-level-friendly roles, and includes skill-assessment pathways so beginners can prove competence before applying — removing the chicken-and-egg problem of needing experience to get experience.
Gap #08
Kitchen Station Hygiene — No Shift Handover Accountability
Consumer 250 signal Quick Win
"That's the current problem I have with my expo station. I go in 30 mins early every day just to clean up after lunch shift."
What's Missing
Enforced cleanup protocols or automated reminders that ensure stations are left ready for the next user. Accountability is entirely culture-dependent — no technical layer enforces or incentivises shift-end cleanliness.
The Opportunity
A simple digital checklist or IoT sensor at kitchen stations that alerts management when end-of-shift cleaning wasn't completed, with a compliance score tied to shift ratings. Low tech, immediate value, replicable across thousands of food service locations.
Gap #09
Sponge & Dishcloth — The Hygiene Loop Problem
Consumer 250 signal Product Gap
"Sponge is dirty, boils sponge to clean. Now pot is dirty, use sponge to clean pot. Goddamn it sponge is dirty again. You're now trapped in a vicious cycle of sponge and pot cleaning."
What's Missing
Materials or designs that resist odour and bacteria buildup, or enable fast, easy sanitisation without creating secondary cleaning obligations. The $14B cleaning products market has not solved the core maintenance loop.
The Opportunity
Antimicrobial sponges or dishcloths with rapid-dry architecture and a clear end-of-life signal (colour change, days indicator). Position directly against the "vicious cycle" with messaging that mirrors exactly how users describe the problem.
Gap #10
Passive Income Platforms — Falsely Passive Promises
Consumer 250 signal Trust Gap
"What's the most realistic passive income stream for beginners today?"
What's Missing
Honest, vetted passive income models with real time-to-maintenance benchmarks. Every platform oversells passivity and undersells ongoing requirements. Users have no way to verify claims before investing significant time.
The Opportunity
A vetted library of passive income models with community-verified maintenance time data, honest setup cost estimates, and income range reporting — anti-guru positioning that builds trust through radical transparency about what "passive" actually means.
Gap #11
Job Postings — Salary Transparency Black Hole
HR & Hiring 100 signal Browser Extension
"Can we normalize putting salary info in job ads?"
What's Missing
Mandatory salary disclosure or a tool that surfaces compensation data from public sources for postings that omit it. Both employers and candidates waste time on mismatched applications when salary is hidden.
The Opportunity
A job-search browser extension that flags or blocks postings without disclosed compensation, overlays Glassdoor/LinkedIn salary data, and sends aggregated signals to employers about how many candidates declined to apply due to missing salary info.
Gap #12
Vet Services — Pet Quality-of-Life Decision Tool
Consumer 500 signal Emotional Gap
"Same here. My cat was diagnosed and then had to be euthanised just two days after his diagnosis. Oral cancer is the worst."
What's Missing
Clear, compassionate tools that help pet owners assess quality-of-life indicators and make timely euthanasia decisions with confidence rather than guilt. Vets provide information but rarely a structured, trackable framework owners can reference over time.
The Opportunity
A vet-provided quality-of-life tracking app or portal that monitors pain, appetite, and mobility metrics over time, offering threshold alerts when indicators suggest euthanasia may be warranted — removing the burden of guessing from grieving pet owners.
Gap #13
App Proliferation — No Optional Web Alternative
Consumer 200 signal Developer SDK
"Not everything needs an app. I hate the appification of everything. No I don't want to give you my email, no I don't want to give you my number, no you do not need to connect to the wifi."
What's Missing
Optional-app pathways or web-based alternatives that deliver core functionality without mandatory data harvesting. Businesses don't have an easy way to offer app-lite experiences even when they want to.
The Opportunity
A developer SDK that enables businesses to offer NFC-based or lightweight web interactions as defaults, with full app features optionally available. A privacy-first infrastructure product that helps businesses retain privacy-sensitive customers.
Gap #14
Tutoring Platforms — Entry Barrier for New Tutors
HR & Hiring 10 signal Access Gap
"Got rejected at Varsity Tutors… Wyzant isn't available in Canada… Dojo rejected again… Tutorlyft rejected (without a demo)… Wiingy rejected… Outschool rejected… It's been a year, still nothing."
What's Missing
Entry pathways for new tutors without prior platform experience. Geographic flexibility for non-US tutors. Alternative assessment methods that don't require live demos with no-show coordinators. The entire market gatekeeps beginners out.
The Opportunity
A tutoring marketplace that offers onboarding micro-credits (free tutoring sessions counted toward experience), accepts international tutors with verified credentials, and replaces live demos with recorded lesson samples — positioning as "the platform that gives you your first chance."
Gap #15
ATS Systems — Killing Applicant Flow at Small Scale
HR & Hiring 9 signal Distribution Gap
"Well since Indeed started to cap free postings to 3, we decided to try an ATS. Since then our applicants have gone down to 0-2 per posting over the last 2 weeks."
What's Missing
ATS configurations that preserve candidate visibility at small-employer scale. Most ATS tools are built for enterprise volume — deployed at SME scale, they create friction that blocks the modest application flow that small employers depend on.
The Opportunity
An ATS-optimised job distribution service that automatically syndicates postings to free boards, social channels, and niche communities while keeping applicant data organised for recruiters — solving both the distribution problem and the management problem simultaneously.
