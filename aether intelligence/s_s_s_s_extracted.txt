##Competitor Gaps
### 1. Bank mobile apps (2FA requiring latest OS)
What users say it gets wrong: ~8 comments / ~610 upvotes – banks lock users out of
accounts if their phone doesn’t support the newest OS version for mandatory app-based
2FA.
The gap: Accessible authentication that works on older devices or offers fallback methods
without forcing upgrades.
> "a few weeks back- they decided to lock anyone who didn't have the newest IOS version
installed on their phone out \"until they update\"."
Opportunity: A 2FA platform that supports SMS, hardware tokens, or web-based verification
alongside app options, ensuring no user is locked out due to OS version.

### 2. Wedding tech SaaS platforms (subscription model aversion)
What users say it gets wrong: ~4 comments / ~500 upvotes – wedding industry customers
reject monthly subscriptions for products needed only for a short event period.
The gap: Pricing models aligned with short-term, event‑based needs rather than recurring
fees.
> "Nobody wants to pay monthly for something they need for 6 months. We almost
launched with a SaaS model. Switched to one-time event passes ($69-$149) and conversions
jumped immediately."
Opportunity: Wedding‑focused tools offered as one‑time event passes or tiered pricing tied
to the event date, eliminating the subscription barrier.

### 3. Subscription/lease models for goods (software, media, car features)
What users say it gets wrong: ~7 comments / ~110 upvotes – previously owned goods are
now leased, costing more and harvesting private data, leaving users feeling they no longer
truly own anything.
The gap: Transparent ownership options or fair‑value lease‑to‑own alternatives that respect
user control and data privacy.
> "Entire things you used to own are now leased to you for more money and your private
data—software, music, video games, features in your new car, etc. It’s actually shameful, but
difficult to fight."
Opportunity: A marketplace or licensing model that lets users purchase perpetual licenses
for software/media or buy out car feature leases after a set period, with clear data‑use
guarantees.

would panic and get frustrated."
