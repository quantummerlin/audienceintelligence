The Humanizer:
Strip AI Tells From Every Output

Anything AI-generated that ships to real humans needs a humanizer pass first. "Unlock," "I hope this finds you well," and "biggest takeaway" are dead giveaways. This skill catches them all — and fixes them automatically before your text ever leaves the editor.

01 Why AI Writing Is Instantly Recognisable

Language models are trained on the entire internet — and the internet is full of corporate press releases, SEO blog posts, and LinkedIn thought leadership. That's the writing style they default to when not told otherwise.

The result is a specific, identifiable texture: overly enthusiastic openers, padded qualifiers, abstract "unlock potential" hype, and bullet points for everything. Readers notice. They don't always consciously label it as AI, but they disengage — the text feels like it was written for no one in particular.

Wikipedia has documented a comprehensive list of signs of AI-generated text. The Humanizer skill is built directly from that list. Run it as the final step before any AI-written text ships to real humans and you eliminate the most obvious giveaways in seconds.

💡
The chain insight: Don't run the Humanizer manually. Build it as a skill in Claude and append → humanize() at the end of every copywriting skill you build. Every email, blog post, and social caption automatically gets a detox pass with zero extra effort.

02 The Complete AI Tells Dictionary

These fall into six categories. The Humanizer skill targets all of them.

Filler Openers
Certainly! Of course! Absolutely! Great question! Definitely! Sure thing!
Vague Power Words
unlock unleash leverage streamline revolutionise game-changer robust seamlessly
Corporate Padding
it's worth noting it goes without saying moving forward at the end of the day in today's world
Email Grease
hope this finds you well I hope you're doing well feel free to reach out please don't hesitate
Over-Hedging
it's important to note please note that as always, consult a professional I must emphasise
Dive/Delve Clichés
dive deep delve into embark on explore cutting-edge groundbreaking
⚠️
The structural tells matter too. Beyond word-level patterns, AI writing has structural tells: bullet-pointing everything that could be prose, adding a "Conclusion" section that repeats the intro, and always ending with "I hope this helps!" or a summary paragraph no one asked for. The Humanizer catches structural patterns, not just word patterns.

04 Build the Humanizer Skill

This is the core skill prompt. Save it as a Claude Project instruction, a Hermes skill file, or any system prompt layer you use. It runs as a final editing pass on any text you feed it.

01

Open Claude and create a new Project

Go to Claude → Projects → New Project. Name it "Humanizer" or add it to an existing writing project as a sub-skill.

02

03

Test it with your own AI-generated text

Skill Prompt — System Instructions 
You are a professional editor who specialises in making AI-generated text
indistinguishable from human writing.

Your task: rewrite the text I give you so it reads as authentic human writing
while preserving every factual claim and the original intent.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT TO FIX — detect and rewrite these AI tells:

1. FILLER OPENERS
   Remove: "Certainly!", "Of course!", "Absolutely!", "Great question!",
   "Sure thing!", "Definitely!", "I'd be happy to..."
   Remove: "I hope this email finds you well", "I hope you're doing well",
   "Feel free to reach out", "Please don't hesitate to contact me"

2. VAGUE POWER WORDS — replace with specific, concrete language
   Remove: unlock, unleash, leverage, streamline, revolutionise, transform,
   empower, harness, elevate, amplify, synergise
   Remove: game-changer, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, innovative solution,
   state-of-the-art, best-in-class, world-class
   Remove: robust, seamlessly, holistically, comprehensively, proactively

3. CORPORATE PADDING — delete entirely
   Remove: "It's worth noting that...", "It goes without saying...",
   "Needless to say...", "It is important to note..."
   Remove: "Moving forward", "Going forward", "At the end of the day",
   "In today's [adjective] world...", "In today's fast-paced landscape..."
   Remove: "The biggest takeaway is...", "It is what it is"

4. STRUCTURAL TELLS
   - If every section has a bullet list, convert some to prose
   - Delete summary paragraphs that repeat what was just said
   - Remove "In conclusion" sections unless the piece genuinely needs one
   - Delete headers on every single paragraph — use them sparingly
   - Remove "I hope this helps!" and any similar sign-off filler

5. DIVE / DELVE CLICHÉS
   Remove: dive deep, delve into, embark on, explore the nuances,
   unpack, breakdown, dissect, navigate (used abstractly)

6. OVER-HEDGING — one qualifier is enough
   Simplify: "It's extremely important to always remember to..."
   Remove triple qualifications: "In some cases, it can sometimes be
   possible that..."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STYLE RULES:
- Preserve ALL factual content — never change meaning
- Keep technical terms that have no cleaner alternative
- Vary sentence length — short punchy sentences work alongside longer ones
- A single sentence can stand alone as its own paragraph for emphasis
- Active voice preferred, but don't force it unnaturally
- The goal is natural, confident, specific — not chatty or slangy

VOICE STYLE:
[Add your voice notes here — e.g. "Dry, direct. Short sentences preferred.
No filler. Occasional wry observation. Never exclamation marks."]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

When finished, output ONLY the rewritten text.
No commentary, no "Here's the humanized version:", no explanation.
Just the clean output.
ℹ️
Hermes / agent skill file: If you're using Hermes Agent or building in Claude Code, wrap this in a Python execute(text: str) -> str function and save as humanizer.py. Register it, then call run_skill("humanizer", text=draft) from inside any other skill.

05 Chain It to Every Copywriting Skill

Running the Humanizer manually every time defeats the point. The real power is wiring it as the automatic final step inside any skill that produces text for external audiences.

To embed the Humanizer inline (without calling it as a separate skill), append this block to the end of any copywriting skill's system prompt:

Inline Chain Snippet 
━━━ FINAL STEP — HUMANIZE BEFORE OUTPUT ━━━
Before producing your final output, scan your draft for:
- Filler openers (Certainly!, Of course!, etc.) — delete them
- Vague power words (unlock, leverage, seamlessly, robust) — replace with specifics
- Corporate padding phrases (it's worth noting, moving forward) — delete
- Repetitive summary paragraphs — delete
- Over-hedged qualifications — simplify to one qualifier maximum
- "Dive deep" / "delve into" type clichés — replace with direct language

Output should read like a sharp human writer. No AI texture.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Python skill chain (Hermes / Claude Code)

Python — skill chaining 
from skill_runner import run_skill

def execute(topic: str, recipient: str, tone: str = "professional") -> dict:
    # Step 1: Write the email
    draft = run_skill("email_composer",
                      topic=topic,
                      recipient=recipient,
                      tone=tone)

    # Step 2: Humanizer pass (always the last step)
    clean = run_skill("humanizer", text=draft["result"]["body"])

    return {
        "status": "success",
        "result": {
            "subject": draft["result"]["subject"],
            "body": clean["result"]
        }
    }

06 Customise With Your Voice Fingerprint

The default Humanizer removes AI texture but doesn't add your texture. That's a separate step — and it's worth doing. Replace the VOICE STYLE placeholder in the skill prompt with a description of how you actually write.

How to capture your voice in 10 minutes

01

Find 5–10 pieces of writing you're proud of

Emails, social posts, Slack messages — anywhere your natural voice came through.

02

Ask Claude to analyse your voice

Paste all 10 samples and prompt: "Analyse these writing samples. Describe my voice in 8–10 specific, concrete bullet points that I could give another writer to replicate my style."

03

Paste the output into the VOICE STYLE section

Keep it brief. Four to six bullets is enough. Claude doesn't need a novel — it needs signal.

Example voice fingerprint

Example — Voice Style Section 
VOICE STYLE:
- Direct and confident. Never hedges more than once.
- Short sentences. Paragraphs rarely exceed 3 sentences.
- Technical but not jargony — explains complex things simply.
- Occasional dry observation. Never sarcastic.
- No exclamation marks. Ever.
- Numbers and specifics preferred over generalities.
- Would rather say "cut your review time by 40%" than "improve efficiency."
💡
Compound over time: Every few months, update your voice fingerprint with new samples. As you write more with AI assistance, your actual voice evolves — keep the skill calibrated to it.
