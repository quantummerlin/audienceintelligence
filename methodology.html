The Process

How I Extract Insights
From Thousands of Comments

A transparent look at the methodology behind Aether Intelligence — from raw data to actionable patterns.

1

Data Collection

I gather publicly available user comments from online discussions — forums, communities, and platforms where real people share real experiences. No private messages, no hacked data, just publicly posted opinions and stories.

  • Only publicly available comments
  • No personal identification extracted
  • Aggregated patterns, not individual tracking
2

Pattern Recognition

Using advanced language models, I analyze thousands of comments to identify recurring themes, pain points, and insights that would be impossible to spot manually. The AI finds patterns — I interpret what they mean.

  • Identify repeated phrases and themes
  • Extract emotional signals and urgency
  • Group related issues into pattern clusters
3

Human Verification

Every pattern is reviewed for accuracy and relevance. I cross-reference findings, verify quotes, and ensure the insights are grounded in real user experiences — not AI hallucinations.

  • Manual review of all extracted insights
  • Quote verification against source
  • Removal of false positives and noise
4

Report Generation

The final output: clear, actionable reports that distill thousands of voices into what actually matters. No fluff, no filler — just signal from noise.

  • Executive summaries for quick insights
  • Detailed breakdowns for deep dives
  • Actionable takeaways you can use
