Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a minimum audience size?

No. We care about quality of recommendations, not audience size. A focused newsletter with 200 engaged readers converts better than a mass channel with 50K passive followers.

How do I get paid?

Payouts are processed monthly via PayPal or bank transfer once you hit a $50 minimum threshold. Commission rates and payout schedules vary by tool partner.

Can I promote tools I haven't tried?

We strongly recommend only promoting tools you've actually used. Authentic reviews convert at 3–5x the rate of generic promotion and build long-term trust with your audience.

Do I need to disclose affiliate relationships?

Yes, and we require it. All affiliate links must be disclosed in accordance with FTC guidelines. We'll provide standard disclosure language you can use.
