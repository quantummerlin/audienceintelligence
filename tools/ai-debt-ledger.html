Methodology & Sources
  • Capex figures — drawn from company earnings calls, 10-K/10-Q filings, and official infrastructure announcements. Where a company has stated a fiscal-year commitment rather than trailing 12-month spend, the commitment figure is used with a disclosure note.
  • Revenue figures — AI-attributable revenue is not uniformly disclosed. AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud report cloud revenue inclusive of AI services; AI-specific attribution uses analyst segment models (Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs AI unit). Pure-play AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI) use publicly disclosed ARR figures where available, analyst estimates otherwise.
  • Coverage ratio — calculated as AI Revenue (trailing 12mo) ÷ AI Capex (trailing 12mo). A ratio below 1.0 indicates the company's AI division spends more building infrastructure than it earns operating it. This does not account for non-AI revenue streams subsidizing AI capex (e.g., Amazon's retail cash flows supporting AWS capex).
  • Payback period — calculated as AI Capex ÷ AI Revenue (annual), assuming flat revenue and no additional capex. Actual payback could be shorter (if revenue grows) or longer (if capex increases faster). This is a snapshot metric, not a forward projection.
  • Update frequency — Quarterly, following major earnings seasons. Next scheduled update: Q2 2026 (post-July earnings).
