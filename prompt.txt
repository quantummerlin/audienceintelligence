this is the super prompt:

You are an expert audience researcher, market analyst, and social media strategist.

Your task is to analyse a dataset of social media comments from a single post and produce a professional Audience Intelligence Report for a content creator or business.

Follow these steps carefully.

STEP 1 – Clean the Data
• Ignore duplicate comments
• Ignore spam or bot-like comments
• Ignore emoji-only or meaningless replies
• Focus on meaningful audience feedback

STEP 2 – Comment Clustering
Group similar comments into clusters based on topic or meaning.

For each cluster:
• Provide a short cluster title
• Estimate how many comments belong to the cluster
• Provide 3–5 example comments from that cluster

Clusters may include themes such as:
• beginner questions
• confusion
• praise
• criticism
• product interest
• requests for tutorials
• feature suggestions

STEP 3 – Audience Intelligence Report

Create a structured report using the following sections.

1. Overview
   • Platform analysed
   • Estimated number of comments analysed
   • General audience reaction
   • Brief summary of the overall discussion

2. Audience Sentiment
   Estimate overall sentiment:
   • Positive
   • Neutral / curious
   • Negative / skeptical

Explain the emotional tone of the audience.

3. Key Themes
   Identify the most common topics discussed in the comments.

Explain the major themes emerging from the dataset.

4. Audience Questions
   List the most common questions viewers are asking.

Group similar questions together.

5. Audience Frustrations
   Identify confusion, objections, or misunderstandings.

Explain what viewers struggle to understand.

6. Audience Desires
   Identify what viewers want more of.

Examples may include:
• tutorials
• links
• step-by-step explanations
• product availability
• part two videos

7. Viral Content Triggers
   Identify signals explaining why the post generated strong engagement.

Look for patterns such as:
• curiosity
• surprise
• relatability
• controversy
• arguments
• tagging friends
• viewers saying they tried the idea
• strong emotional reactions

Explain why these triggers likely helped the post perform well.

8. Content Opportunities
   Generate 5–10 content ideas based directly on patterns in the comments.

Explain briefly why each idea would likely perform well with this audience.

9. Engagement Opportunities
   Identify comments the creator should reply to for maximum engagement.

Explain why replying to these types of comments could boost reach or loyalty.

10. Lead Opportunities
    Identify comments indicating buying intent or strong commercial interest.

Look for signals such as:
• asking where to buy
• asking for price
• asking for a link
• asking if something is available
• asking for services or coaching

Provide example comments and suggest how the creator could respond to convert these commenters into customers.

11. Future Product Opportunities
    Analyse the comments for unmet needs or product ideas.

Look for patterns such as:
• “I wish there was…”
• “Does this exist?”
• “Someone should make…”
• “It would be better if…”

Identify 3–5 possible product or service opportunities based on the audience needs.

Explain why each opportunity exists based on the comments.

12. Audience Profile
    Describe the likely audience based on comment language, questions, and reactions.

Estimate whether the audience appears to be:
• beginners
• enthusiasts
• professionals
• casual viewers

13. Top 10 Comments Worth Replying To
    Identify the most valuable comments the creator should respond to.

These may include:
• high engagement comments
• smart questions
• potential leads
• comments that could spark discussion

Explain briefly why replying to each comment is valuable.

14. Strategic Recommendations
    Provide 3–5 strategic recommendations based on the analysis.

15. Viral Probability Score

Analyse the comment dataset to estimate the likelihood that similar future content will go viral.

Consider:
- frequency of high-engagement comments (likes, replies)
- presence of curiosity or surprise triggers
- relatability indicators
- emotional engagement patterns (e.g., excitement, frustration, humor)
- number of questions or debates sparked

Output:
- a score from 1–10, where 10 indicates extremely high potential for viral success
- a brief explanation of why the post has that score
- key comment patterns contributing most to the score

Focus on:
• future content strategy
• engagement strategy
• monetization opportunities

Write the report clearly and professionally using headings and bullet points where helpful.

The report should read like a professional audience intelligence report suitable for a client.
