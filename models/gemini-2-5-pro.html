What is Gemini 2.5 Pro?

Gemini 2.5 Pro is Google's state-of-the-art AI model designed for advanced reasoning, coding, mathematics, and scientific tasks. Launched in June 2025, it operates with a 1,048,576-token (~1M) context window and natively processes five modalities in a single request: text, images, audio, video, and files. That combination of depth and breadth puts it in a category of its own within the Google model family — capable of working through research papers, long code repositories, or 90-minute audio recordings without switching tools or losing context.

The headline architectural feature is thinking mode. Rather than generating a response immediately, Gemini 2.5 Pro first produces a visible internal reasoning chain — a stream of deliberate cognition that works through the problem before committing to an answer. This front-loaded reasoning dramatically improves accuracy on complex multi-step tasks, but it consumes tokens. In our benchmark at 650 max_tokens, the model spent 623 of those 646 completion tokens on visible thinking — leaving only 23 tokens for the actual response text. In production, set max_tokens to 2,000–8,000+ to receive a full answer.

Thinking Mode — Important for Developers

Gemini 2.5 Pro's thinking tokens count against your max_tokens budget. If you cap a request at 650 tokens, almost all of them will be consumed by internal reasoning before a single word of visible response is written. Always allocate a generous token budget — 2,000 tokens minimum for simple queries, 8,000+ for complex analysis or multi-part answers.
