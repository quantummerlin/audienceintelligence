Critical Insight: The RAI Gap
The most strategically significant finding across all batches is the difference between political Facebook audiences and the RAI YouTube audience.

Political Facebook (Batches 1-3): 91-93% support, 1-5% opposition — this audience is self-selecting. People who follow political pages are predisposed to agree with the post. While the numbers are impressive, critics can dismiss them as "echo chamber" effects.

RAI YouTube (Batch 4): 82% support, 10% skeptical, 5% pro-separation — this audience is the general public. RAI is Italy's public broadcaster with 6.5 million subscribers. These are not political followers — they are ordinary Italians watching the news. The support rate dropping to 82% actually makes it MORE credible, not less. It represents genuine mainstream consensus.

For the campaign: Always lead with the RAI numbers when speaking to media or legal professionals. "82% of viewers of Italy's national public broadcaster supported the family after seeing the footage" is a far more powerful statement than "93% of a politician's followers agreed."

The RAI footage of Catherine's removal was a turning point. Before the footage, comments were sympathetic. After the footage, comments became accusatory ("crime of state", "kidnapping", "violence"). Visual evidence is more powerful than any written account.

Trigger 7: "They Were Happy" Narrative (Score: 7/10)
"Erano felici li hanno distrutti" — the before/after narrative is incredibly powerful. The audience has seen the family's happy life and now sees the destruction. This contrast drives emotional engagement.

Trigger 8: The Converted Skeptic (Score: 7/10)
