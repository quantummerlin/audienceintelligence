## What It Does

This agent helps you build and run an AI agent management business — the model where one person charges $3,000-$7,000/month per client to deploy and maintain AI agents for professional service businesses.

The playbook inside this agent comes from the builders who are actually running this business today. One source: a full course episode on Greg Isenberg's podcast featuring Nick from Orgo, who broke down exactly how to structure the offer, which industries to target, what to charge, and how to onboard a client in 48 hours. This agent operationalizes that playbook.

---

## The Model in Brief

**What you sell:** Not agents. Not tokens. Not software. A digital employee. The client doesn't touch infrastructure. They get a result.

**What you charge:** $5K/month is the working sweet spot for professional service firms. Some operators go lower for small businesses, some go higher for enterprise.

**Who you sell to:** Marketing agencies, law firms, insurance agencies, real estate agencies, manufacturers, wholesalers. In that order of least friction.

**How fast to deliver:** First agent live in 48 hours. This creates momentum before doubt sets in.

## What the Agent Helps With

- Choosing your target industry and niche
- Structuring your offer so clients say yes without asking about tokens
- Writing pitches for specific client types (law firm partner, agency owner)
- Designing the 30-day onboarding flow
- Picking the right tech stack for each use case
- Handling difficult conversations when an agent underperforms

## Quick Start

1. Import `solo-agent-business.json` into Hyperagent — or paste the system prompt into a Claude Project
2. Tell the agent where you are: "I have some savings and want to start this business but no clients yet" or "I have one client and want to productize the offer"
3. The agent will ask you questions and build your specific playbook
